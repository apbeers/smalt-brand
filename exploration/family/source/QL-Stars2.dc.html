Making the star read as *

Q4's layout, kept: separate arms around a chip in the centre. What changes is the spacing: Q4 had arms at 90° and 45°, so the gaps were uneven and it didn't read as *. T1–T3 space six arms 60° apart, as the family fonts draw the * (left); they differ in weight, gap and chip size. T4 keeps the 0/45/90° rule with eight arms.
