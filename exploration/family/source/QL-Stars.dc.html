Making the star read as *

Q4 didn't read as an asterisk: its arms didn't meet and weren't evenly spaced. A * has six arms 60° apart (left, as the family fonts draw it), and the family's 0/45/90° rule can only space arms evenly in fours or eights. S1 and S2 bend the rule to 60° for this mark; S3 and S4 keep it with eight arms. The chip is either the lower arm or the centre. Shown in leaf green (V1) as a placeholder.
