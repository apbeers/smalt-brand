A mark for the people who make the smalt family

A smalt works was a factory that made the pigment: glass melted in a kiln and ground to powder. These marks draw on that, by the family rules: a smalt body and one chip. smaltworks has no product colour, so the chip is shown in kiln orange, the brand's existing accent (kiln-500). The colour panel compares other choices.
