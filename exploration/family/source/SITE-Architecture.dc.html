One engine, many languages, three apps

The stack as described, bottom up: sources (OPFS, external data, plugins) feed one engine, smaltdb. SQL and smaltdf are two languages on that same engine. Kits sit above them, and smaltui, smaltbi and smaltnb are built on the kits. smaltkit has no mark of its own, so the kits layer is words only.
